# Sourcing and outreach

Two halves of one lens: see what changes around you, and be the one who reaches out first.

## Sourcing: industry and partnerships

Inputs: `watchlist.md` (companies, people, topics), CRM partner-type records, web search and research tools if connected, `resources/` industry notes.

1. **Signals.** For each watchlist item, at most one new dated public signal since the last run: leadership change, funding, launch, hiring, event, regulation. Cite source and date. No signal is a valid result.
2. **Partner fit score** (1 to 5 each, for candidate partners): audience overlap (do they reach who we want?), complementarity (do they do what we do not?), reach (size relative to us), warmth (do we know anyone there?), effort (how hard to start?). Fit = overlap + complementarity + reach + warmth − effort. Show the five numbers, not just the total.
3. **Shortlist.** Monthly, the top five candidates with fit score, the why-now signal if any, and the person to approach (role level; name only if public or already in `people/`).
4. Write back to `watchlist.md` (last signal, date) and add new candidates to `matrix.md` as Sourcing items for scoring.

## Outreach: proactive, with a reason

Inputs: `people/` files (role, why they matter, last contact, cadence), `outreach/queue.md`, `outreach/log.md`, calendar (met recently, meeting soon), mailbox (threads going cold), CRM.

1. **Warmth and cadence.** For each person: days since last contact vs their cadence (default: partners 30 days, mentors 45, peers 60, customers per account plan). Overdue = candidate.
2. **Reason.** A real one, dated: something they did, something you can offer, a signal from the sourcing half, a meeting coming up, a thank-you owed. No reason, no outreach.
3. **Three names a week.** Rank candidates by (overdue × reason strength × matrix horizon). Three, not ten.
4. **Draft.** For each: channel (email, message, LinkedIn-type note), three to six sentences in the user's voice, the reason in the first line, one specific ask or offer. Mark "not sent".
5. **Queue and log.** Refill `outreach/queue.md` with the three plus two reserves. When the user says they sent one, log it in `outreach/log.md` with the date and update `people/<name>.md` last contact.

## Output format

```
Sourcing · {date}
Signals: {company} · {signal} · {source, date} · action: {one line}   (or: no new signals)
Partner shortlist (monthly): {name} · fit {total} ({overlap}/{complementarity}/{reach}/{warmth}/{effort}) · approach: {role}

Outreach · week of {date}
1. {name} · {role, company} · last contact {d} days ago (cadence {c}) · reason: {dated} · draft below (not sent)
2. ...
3. ...
Reserves: {two names}
Prepared for you: {three drafts}
```

## Rules

- Never send, connect, post or book. The user does. Then tell the brain, and it logs it.
- Public information about people: professional role and public professional activity only, cited with source and date. Nothing else.
- A reason is dated and specific or it is not a reason.
- Three names a week. Reserves wait.
